Privacy Policy

Effective: February 10, 2026

SensorBar collects absolutely no data. Period.

No Data Collection

SensorBar does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information. Everything stays on your Mac.

No Analytics or Tracking

There are no analytics frameworks, no tracking pixels, no telemetry, and no crash reporters. We have zero insight into how you use the app — and that's by design.

No Network Calls

SensorBar makes no network connections whatsoever. It reads your system sensors locally and displays them in your menu bar. That's it.

No Accounts

There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no logins. You download it and use it.

No Third-Party SDKs

SensorBar contains no third-party SDKs, frameworks, or libraries that could collect data on our behalf.

Your Data

All system information (CPU usage, temperatures, fan speeds, etc.) is read directly from your Mac's hardware sensors and displayed locally. This data never leaves your computer.
