Frequently Asked Questions

What are the system requirements?
SensorBar requires macOS 13 (Ventura) or later.
How do I show or hide modules?
Click the SensorBar icon in your menu bar to open the dropdown, then use the settings to toggle individual modules (CPU, Memory, Network, Disk, Battery, Sensors) on or off.
Does SensorBar affect battery life?
SensorBar is designed to be lightweight. It uses minimal CPU and energy. You can also adjust the update interval in settings to further reduce resource usage.
Does SensorBar collect any data?
No. SensorBar collects zero data, makes no network connections, and contains no analytics or tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.
How do I uninstall SensorBar?
Simply drag SensorBar from your Applications folder to the Trash, or right-click and select "Move to Trash."
